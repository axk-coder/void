Privacy Policy

Data Collection

void operates strictly on a client-side architecture. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or monitor any personal user data, browsing activity, or device identifiers.

Local Storage

User preferences such as tab cloaking configurations and panic button settings are stored exclusively in your local browser storage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

External Content

When interactive content or external resources are loaded, those services may operate under their own independent policies. We enforce sandboxing constraints to restrict unauthorized storage or data transmission from framed content.

Security

We apply defensive headers, referrer restrictions, and sandboxing rules to minimize exposure and protect local environment integrity.
